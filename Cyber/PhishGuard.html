What is this simulator?

PhishGuard is a security-awareness training application. It uses fictional scenarios to teach people how to identify suspicious messages and social-engineering techniques.

What should I do when I see phishing?

  • Stop before clicking.
  • Inspect the sender and request.
  • Verify unusual requests through a trusted channel.
  • Report suspicious messages according to your organization's process.
Never enter real passwords, MFA codes, payment information, or other secrets into a training simulation.
